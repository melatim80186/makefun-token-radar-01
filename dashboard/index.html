How to farm it

    How the value is built

    Every number below comes from the snapshot's own figures — no list of names, no outside price. A protocol's implied value is what its token would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is.

    Multiple. For each category, the peers are the protocols in that category whose token already trades and whose market cap is recorded: their market-cap-to-TVL ratio, capped at 2.0×, and the median of those ratios is the category benchmark. A category with few peers is shrunk toward the market median (0.2138×) with a prior weight of k = 3 — so a multiple resting on one peer is pulled most of the way toward the market, and a multiple resting on many peers stands on its own.

    Implied value. TVL × multiple × growth × maturity × chain reach. Growth is the seven-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100% and halved. Maturity is 0.6 plus up to 0.4 for up to three years tracked with no token. Chain reach is 1 plus 0.02 per extra chain, up to five.

    Thin evidence, stated plainly. Risk Curators rests on 1 peer (its ratio is capped at 2.0×); Prediction Market rests on 1 peer whose recorded ratio is 400× and is likewise capped at 2.0×. Categories with no live peer at all use the market median. The multiple shown for each protocol names its peer count, so a reader can see how much evidence stands behind it.

    What the board leaves out, and why. A protocol is ranked only if it records no token. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of a token that already trades are set aside, as are exchanges and version-marked copies — they are not launches. Live-token protocols are listed under Already trading, never ranked.
