How the implied value is built

implied = TVL × multiple × (1 + ½ of the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100%) × (0.6 + age up to 3 years × 0.4) × (1 + 2% per extra chain, up to five)

The multiple is the category's own FDV/TVL: the median of its tokenized protocols' market-cap/TVL ratios, each ratio capped at 2.0× first, shrunk toward the market median with three peers' weight, capped at 1.0× and floored at 0.02×. A category with no tokenized peer takes the market median. Nothing is hardcoded by protocol name.
